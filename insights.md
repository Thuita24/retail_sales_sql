### Insight 1: High-Value Customers

**Finding:** Brian Chebet is the highest-spending customer, generating KSh 848,118.75 from completed orders. The top 10 customers each generated more than KSh 664,000 in sales.

**Why it matters:** High-spending customers are an important source of revenue. Retaining these customers can support consistent sales and long-term business growth.

**Recommendation:** Introduce a customer loyalty programme, personalised promotions and targeted retention strategies to encourage repeat purchases among high-value customers. 

### Insight 2: Product Category Profitability

**Finding:** Mobile Phones generated the highest revenue (KSh 6,942,060.65) and total profit (KSh 728,760.65). However, Accessories recorded the highest profit margin at 42.39%, despite generating the lowest revenue of KSh 1,077,496.10. Computing ranked second in both revenue and total profit.

**Why it matters:** The results demonstrate that the categories generating the most revenue are not necessarily the ones with the highest profit margins. Understanding this difference helps management make better inventory, pricing and purchasing decisions.

**Recommendation:** Maintain sufficient inventory of high-performing categories such as Mobile Phones and Computing. Investigate the strong margins in Accessories and explore opportunities to grow its sales. Management should also review supplier costs and discount policies in lower-margin categories to improve profitability.

### Insight 3: Revenue Performance by Customer City

**Finding:** Nairobi generated the highest revenue at KSh 9,581,980.35, followed by Mombasa at KSh 3,161,889.60 and Nakuru at KSh 2,391,328. Machakos recorded the lowest revenue at KSh 745,099.90.

**Why it matters:** The results reveal that sales are heavily concentrated in Nairobi, making it the business's strongest market. Revenue differences across cities may indicate variations in customer demand and market penetration.

**Recommendation:** Maintain strong operations in high-performing markets while introducing targeted marketing campaigns in lower-revenue cities. Management should also compare revenue against customer counts by city to identify opportunities for customer acquisition and increased spending.

### Insight 4: Top Revenue-Generating Products

**Finding:** The iPhone 13 generated the highest revenue at KSh 3,766,458.15, followed by the HP 250 G9 Laptop at KSh 2,510,510.15 and the Hotpoint Refrigerator at KSh 2,386,956.60. The Lenovo IdeaPad 3 and Samsung 55in Smart TV completed the top five.

**Why it matters:** The results identify products that contribute significantly to business revenue. Understanding which products perform best helps management make informed inventory and purchasing decisions.

**Recommendation:** Maintain adequate stock of high-performing products, negotiate favourable supplier prices and monitor demand to prevent stock shortages. Management should also evaluate product-level profit margins before prioritising products based on revenue alone.

### Insight 5: Cumulative Monthly Revenue Performance

**Finding:** Cumulative revenue increased consistently from KSh 434,902.70 in January 2025 to KSh 24,155,371.70 by September 2026. Monthly revenue generally increased over the period, with August 2026 recording the highest monthly revenue of KSh 2,242,033.90.

**Why it matters:** Cumulative revenue provides management with a broader view of business performance and helps track revenue growth over time. The upward trend suggests improving sales performance across the reporting period.

**Recommendation:** Investigate the factors driving strong monthly sales, particularly in August 2026 and December 2025. Use these findings to guide sales planning, establish monthly revenue targets and develop strategies to improve performance during weaker months.
